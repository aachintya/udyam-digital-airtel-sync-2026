3. **Gemini and architecture.** Separate consent, live needs discovery and structured product proposals. Explicit numeric facts are reconciled locally. Required per-goal selections prevent silent omission; catalogue rules set prices and validate quantities, eligibility and dependencies.

Setup INR 1,770, first invoice INR 6,242.20, minimum term 12 months. Against an entered INR 6,000 budget, headroom is INR 1,527.80. This is not a proven saving.
5. **Smart KYC and privacy.** Actual local OCR, synthetic GST/Udyam lookups, name/address/date checks, separate AI/KYC consent, temporary uploads, 24-hour inactivity expiry and withdrawal deletion. Authenticity and statutory compliance are not certified.
6. **Partners and rollout.** Contextual handoffs and recorded review, scheduling and simulated activation. Weeks 1–2 validate rules/KYC, weeks 3–6 train partners and compare cohorts, weeks 7–12 integrate providers and secure identity. Risks: serviceability, identity mismatch, data leakage and channel conflict. Gates: site checks, review, authenticated roles/encryption and joint channel attribution.
7. **Pilot outcomes and proof.** Targets: conversion 12% from 7.36%, activation under 7 days from 17.6, CAC under INR 10,000 from INR 15,053, calls under 1 from 3, churn no worse than the 15.2% baseline. These are hypotheses. At equal lead quality, 600 activations would be 232 above baseline. Measure margin, installation cost, commissions and retention before claiming profit.
