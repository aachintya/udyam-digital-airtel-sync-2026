# Live walkthrough and voiceover guide

Watch the [final narrated demo](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1kDy5QKG6w8ETujzBu6YHIMM5fdb2Mt4M/view?usp=sharing), with subtitles and Gemini-generated male Indian English narration. Runtime: 2:48. The original silent video remains available in release v1.0. The [timed voiceover script](../submission/Voiceover-Script.md) is included for reference.

1. Open Overview, then Business advisor. Enable Gemini separately from KYC permission.
2. Describe a clinic with eight staff at one location. Let Gemini ask about current tools and goals.
3. Add home broadband/personal email today, internet/email/device security/failover needs, eight users/devices and a tax-inclusive INR 6,000 budget. Review the structured summary. Exact stated counts and budget are reconciled locally.
4. Build the plan. Gemini proposes one product for every requested goal. The local engine validates the quote. Edit one email user and observe the recalculated bill, then restore eight.
5. Verification: give KYC consent and try the K02 synthetic business. Run actual OCR for certificate, address and signatory specimens.
6. Optionally inspect the K01 certificate exception, then restore K02. Automated checks support partner review, not authenticity certification.
7. Review the monthly bill, setup fee and first invoice. Reserve the demo plan. No payment/contract is created.
8. Open demo operations. Record a review note, schedule a date and record simulated completion. Every transition requires a note.
9. Return to the tracker and reload to show persistence.
10. Show English/Hindi phone layouts and the partner desk's actual 5,000-lead analytics.

When Gemini is rate-limited or unavailable, the app reports the failure and preserves the previous plan and input. Retry later or choose labelled guided mode. Never present guided output as a live model response. Do not reveal your API key during screen sharing.
