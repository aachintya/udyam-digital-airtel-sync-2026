# Privacy and security design note

**Purpose and consent.** The app asks for business category, team/device/site counts, priorities and budget to recommend services. Registration lookup and document extraction require affirmative consent for this demo order. Pausing consent blocks further verification/progression. Withdrawal deletes the current workspace, its orders and help requests.

**Minimisation.** Registration identifiers are used transiently for exact synthetic-registry lookup, then stored as a hash and masked display value. Only business name, registered business address, status, extracted signatory name/document date and check results are retained for review. Identifier entry is masked and cleared after lookup. Request logging is disabled. Original identity-document numbers are not retained.

**Documents.** PNG/JPEG files are processed locally with Apple Vision or Tesseract. Uploaded files live in an automatically removed temporary directory during extraction. Source images are not kept in the workspace or sent to a cloud OCR service. Colour filtering and text alignment improve OCR readability, not authenticity. Unreadable/mismatched evidence requires correction and partner review. The supplied fixtures use 15 July 2026 for date checks.

**Retention and control.** A random HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookie scopes a browser workspace. Records expire after 24 hours of inactivity; cleanup runs at startup and on incoming workspace requests. Explicit withdrawal cascades deletion immediately. SQLite is local and not encrypted; deleting records is not guaranteed forensic erasure and does not manage operating-system backups. Production needs documented backup expiry and erasure.

**Gemini and separate permission.** Gemini assistance requires a separate opt-in from KYC. The current needs message and allowlisted sizing, goals, budget and current-tool categories go to Google. Common identifier/contact patterns are redacted, but this cannot guarantee anonymity. Owners are instructed to omit personal details. Documents, registry records and stored names/addresses are excluded from requests. Google processes requests under the configured account terms; this prototype makes no zero-retention claim. Disabling Gemini stops further AI calls. Schema and local catalogue checks constrain proposals; the model cannot approve KYC or activate an order. API keys stay in the server environment or an ignored local `.env`.

**Security boundary.** The app binds to loopback. Mutations require same-origin JSON; order access is session-scoped; activation transitions require consent and an audit note. The partner role is explicitly a local simulation. Production requires authenticated roles, encrypted storage, managed secrets, malware/rate-limit controls, signed provider webhooks, consent audit evidence, rights/grievance procedures, incident response and legal review. Use only the supplied fictional data. This prototype is not a claim of DPDP or telecom compliance.
